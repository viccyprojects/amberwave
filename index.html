What we do

Two doors. One label.

Whether you're not ready to sign yet or you're ready right now, there's a track built for where you are.

Scout & Market

We find artists early and build real momentum around them — release strategy, playlist pitching, short-form content, and fan-growth campaigns — with no contract required. You keep full ownership of your masters.

Amberwave Records

Our in-house label for artists who are ready to sign. Distribution, A&R development, budget for releases, and a team that already knows your catalogue from the marketing stage.

Process

From first upload to signed artist.

01

Submit

Send us your name, your platform, and a link to your profile. Two minutes, no gatekeeping.

02

Scout review

Our scouting team listens, checks your catalogue and growth, and maps where you're strongest.

03

Marketing sprint

We run a real campaign behind your next release — playlists, content, press — before any contract talk.

04

Amberwave Records

If it's a fit on both sides, you're invited to sign with Amberwave Records on your terms.
