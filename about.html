About Amberwave

Marketing comes
before the ink dries.

Most labels find you after you've already built an audience. We'd rather help you build it — then talk about signing.

The problem

Most artists get signed, then marketed. We do it backwards.

The traditional path asks an unsigned artist to prove themselves alone first, then hands them a marketing team only after a contract is signed. By then, most of the real growth work — the playlist pitching, the content strategy, the audience building — should already be underway. Amberwave starts there instead.

How we're different

Two teams, one artist file.

Our scouting team and our label team share the same notes — nothing gets re-explained when you move from one door to the other.

Scouting team

Listens to every submission personally, tracks growth across platforms, and builds a real campaign — playlisting, short-form content, press outreach — around artists who aren't signed to anyone.

Amberwave Records

Our label arm. When an artist is ready, they apply directly — same form, same team, no cold intros. Distribution, release budget, and A&R support that already understands the catalogue.

No lock-in scouting

Being scouted and marketed by Amberwave never requires signing anything. You keep your masters and your independence for as long as you want them.

Platform-agnostic

We work with artists on Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, SoundCloud, Audiomack, Boomplay, and beyond — wherever your audience already lives.

What we look for

Amberwave, not just noise.
