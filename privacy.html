Privacy & cookies.

The short version: we don't sell your data, and we don't run ad trackers. Here's what we actually do.

What cookies we use

A small local cookie/localStorage flag to remember you've dismissed the cookie notice, and one to remember a manually chosen site language. Neither is used for advertising or shared with third parties.

What we collect through forms

When you apply to Amberwave Records or send us a message, we store what you submit (name, contact info, links, and any verification photos on the application form) so our scouting team can review it. This is only ever seen by our team — it's never posted publicly or sold.
